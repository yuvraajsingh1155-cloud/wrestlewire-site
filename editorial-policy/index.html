Editorial Standards & Corrections

Source attribution, cautious claims and visible corrections come first.

Primary sources

We use official promotion statements and publisher-uploaded videos for facts those sources can substantiate: publication of an upload, announced match cards and disclosed event details. A video title alone does not prove results or backstage claims.

Reports versus storylines

Professional wrestling combines scripted storytelling with real athletic risks. On-screen injuries, suspensions or character departures are not treated as medical or contractual facts without separate trustworthy confirmation.

Business and earnings numbers

We distinguish business revenue from profit, reported attendance from tickets sold and wrestler salary estimates from actual publicly disclosed contracts. We do not invent private salaries or revenue statistics.

AI and automation

Automated Source Radar entries and short source briefs use publisher video metadata only. Metadata briefs are marked as such and not indexed by search engines. Optional AI-generated article drafts require human fact-checking and editorial review before any full reported article is published. AI output must not be presented as independently verified reporting.

Licensing and credits

Any photo used on the site should have a suitable license and attribution. We link to the original source where possible rather than copying copyrighted articles, transcripts or official branding without permission.

Corrections

To request a correction, identify the page, the exact disputed statement and supporting evidence through the issue tracker linked on our Contact page. Public corrections should be made clearly and promptly when evidence warrants a change.
