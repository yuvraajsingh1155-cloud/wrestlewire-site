About WrestleWire

An independent, source-first wrestling publication project.

What we cover

WrestleWire is an independent website about professional wrestling, including WWE, AEW, major wrestling performers, wrestler career histories, wrestling culture and the business behind televised events. We aim to make reliable information easier to find and distinguish documented facts from opinion or speculation.

How the site is funded

The site currently runs on GitHub and Cloudflare under their available free service allowances. We have not activated any paid AI article-generation subscription or advertisements. We may consider advertising after the editorial library becomes more substantial and a review is completed; placement or approval is not guaranteed.

News and sources

Our Official Source Radar links to videos uploaded to the verified WWE and AEW YouTube channels. These linked publisher titles are not original reporting. Separate wrestling explainers are written for context, while automatic metadata briefs explicitly label the limited claims they can support.

Independence

WrestleWire is not part of WWE, AEW or another wrestling company and is not endorsed by any performer or promotion. Editorial views belong to the site and should not be confused with statements by linked sources.
