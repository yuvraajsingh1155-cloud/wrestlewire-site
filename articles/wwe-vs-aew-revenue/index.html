WWE vs AEW Revenue: Which Figures Are Really Comparable?

Revenue, profit, rights fees and ticket gates are not interchangeable.

WRESTLEWIRE EDITORIAL · 22 November 2026 · ORIGINAL EXPLAINER

Comparing WWE with AEW is one of professional wrestling's most popular business debates. But a fair comparison begins by defining the measures. Revenue is the money recognized from business activity; profit is what remains after applicable costs. Neither figure can be inferred from social-media engagement.

WWE's business can be studied through public reports from TKO Group Holdings, which also includes other operations. Analysts should identify the relevant segment, the reporting period and any changes in accounting definitions before drawing conclusions about WWE alone.

AEW does not provide public financial disclosure in the same manner, so direct comparisons with a selected figure from TKO may be misleading. A rumored contract value or estimated TV rights agreement should be labeled as reported or estimated, never as an audited fact.

Live-event gates and television audience figures are useful but narrower measurements. A strong business article makes its sources and caveats visible and avoids declaring a financial winner when the comparable evidence is missing.
