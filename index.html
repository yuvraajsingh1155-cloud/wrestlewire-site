ABOUT THE NEWSROOM

Who we are

WrestleWire is an independent, pre-launch wrestling publication project focused on WWE, AEW, wrestling culture and the business of professional wrestling. We are not affiliated with any promotion or performer. Current feature articles are labeled editorial demos.

Official Source Radar shows attributed video titles and links, not original reporting.

Editorial & corrections

We separate official announcements, independently corroborated reports, opinion and unverified speculation. We do not invent injuries, private salaries, attendance or company revenue figures.

If an error is spotted, provide the article title, disputed statement and source evidence via the project’s issue tracker. Submissions may be public. Corrections will be attributed or noted where appropriate.

Privacy & contact

The current demonstration has no user account, payment form, newsletter signup or site-run advertising script. Hosting and linked external services may independently process visitor information under their own policies.

Do not post private personal information in public GitHub issues. Before analytics, newsletter or ads are introduced, this notice must be updated to reflect those services and applicable consent requirements.
